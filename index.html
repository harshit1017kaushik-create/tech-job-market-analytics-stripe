05

What the analysis found

Six takeaways from the snapshot.

Sales-heavy

Sales / Business Development leads with 173 postings (24.03%), ahead of Software Engineering at 128 (17.78%).

Concentrated top

The top three categories, adding Strategy / Operations (83), make up 43.34% of postings.

Global footprint

Singapore (50), Dublin (46), Bengaluru (34) and London (32) rank ahead of any US city.

SQL over Python

SQL is mentioned in 150 postings versus 87 for Python. Only Data Science shows an even split.

Analytics teams want both

Security Analytics leads both counts (14 Python, 13 SQL). Sales Operations asks for SQL (10) but never Python.

Seniority is murky

417 postings have no detectable level, a classification limit that better text rules could improve.

Caveats

  • Snapshot, not a live count. Listings change over time; this is not Stripe's current vacancy total.
  • Keyword mentions ≠ requirements. A skill flag means the word appeared in the description.
  • Seniority is rule-based and most postings fall into Unspecified.
  • Locations are raw values. Spelling variants and combined locations are counted separately.
  • date_updated is the last-update timestamp, not the original publish date.
